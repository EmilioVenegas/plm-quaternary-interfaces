# Prior study: metal-coordination chemistry vs positional conservation

Provenance note. This repository continues a completed, pre-registered study that
returned a clean negative. That study is not re-run here; this note records what it
asked, how it was run, what it found, and — most importantly — the coverage failure it
uncovered, which is what the PTM design was built to avoid.

Source of every number below: the module docstring of
`scripts/metal_coordination_confound.py` (executed and verified against real data on
2026-08-28). Nothing here is reconstructed from memory.

## The question

Do zero-shot protein language models encode **metal-coordination chemistry**, or only
**generic positional conservation**?

Operationalised (H1): at metal-coordinating positions, a PLM should overestimate the
fitness of substitutions that preserve the donor-atom chemistry — because the model has
learned "this column is conserved" rather than "this side chain donates a thiolate to a
zinc". If that is metal-specific, the effect must be *selectively worse* at metal
positions than at a matched non-metal catalytic control. The falsification rule and the
significance threshold (p < 0.01) were fixed before the test was run.

## Pipeline in brief

1. **Assay selection** — ProteinGym v1.3 reference metadata (`DMS_substitutions.csv`)
   → 6 metalloenzyme DMS assays + TEM-1 β-lactamase (class A *serine* mechanism, no
   metal) as a fold-matched non-metal catalytic control.
2. **Structural ground truth** — real holo/apo PDB entries fetched from RCSB.
   Coordinating/catalytic residues identified by distance (≤ 3.0 Å, donor-atom aware,
   BioLiP/MetalPDB convention) and cross-checked against published biochemistry.
3. **Coordinate mapping** — PDB numbering mapped onto ProteinGym `target_seq` indexing
   by pairwise sequence alignment, never by assumed offset. This caught two real
   mismatches: calmodulin (+1 offset, likely a non-human construct) and BLAT_ECOLX
   (Ambler numbering ≠ raw sequential index).
4. **Scoring** — ESM2-650M (`facebook/esm2_t33_650M_UR50D`) masked-marginal log-odds at
   every verified position.
5. **Classification** — substitutions labelled compatible/incompatible by donor-chemistry
   family: thiolate {C}, imidazole {H}, carboxylate {D,E,N,Q}, hydroxyl {S,T,Y}, amine
   {K,R} — applied identically at metal and control positions.
6. **Primary test** — stratified permutation test (10,000 shuffles) on the interaction
   statistic: (compatible − incompatible residual gap at metal positions) − (the same gap
   at control positions). Residual = within-assay z-scored DMS score − within-assay
   z-scored zero-shot score.
7. **Negative controls** — permuted-weight ESM2 (is the baseline correlation *learned*?)
   and shuffled `is_metal` labels (is the result an artifact of position selection?).

## Result

- **Baseline validity check.** The zero-shot score correlates with real DMS fitness at
  both metal positions (Spearman **ρ = 0.42, p = 1.5 × 10⁻²³, n = 528**) and control
  positions (**ρ = 0.18, p = 2.0 × 10⁻³, n = 284**). The model captures real signal, so a
  null on the primary test cannot be dismissed as a broken measurement.
- **Primary interaction test.** Observed statistic **−0.24**, permutation
  **p = 0.42** — **not significant** at the pre-registered threshold p < 0.01.
- **Direction check.** Of the 4 metal proteins with a usable compatible/incompatible
  contrast, all 4 show the *same sign* as the non-metal control. No metal-specific
  divergence.
- **Permuted-weight control held.** With randomised ESM2 weights the baseline correlation
  collapses (**ρ 0.31 → 0.10**), confirming the signal is learned rather than
  architectural; the interaction statistic remains non-significant there too (p = 0.085).
- **Shuffled-label control held.** The true-label statistic sits at the **45th
  percentile** of the null generated by randomising metal/control identity — the position
  selection is not manufacturing significance.

## Conclusion

**H1 is not supported.** The pattern "PLM zero-shot overestimates fitness for
family-conservative substitutions" is real in the data, but it is *not* selectively worse
at metal-coordinating positions than at a matched non-metal catalytic control. It looks
like a general property of highly conserved, functionally essential positions, not
evidence that PLMs specifically conflate coordination chemistry with generic
conservation. A clean negative, with both negative controls behaving as designed.

## Documented caveats

1. **GAL4's deposited structure (`1D66`) contains Cd²⁺, not Zn²⁺** in the Zn₂Cys₆
   cluster. This is a standard crystallographic surrogate with the same 6-Cys
   coordination geometry, not a data defect. It was documented rather than silently
   substituted.
2. **CYP2C9 and GAL4 contribute ZERO compatible (family-preserving) substitutions.**
   Their donor chemistry is Cys-only, and the thiolate family {C} has a single member —
   so no natural-amino-acid substitution other than the wild type itself preserves the
   family. This is a real structural fact, not a bug, and it cut the usable metal-protein
   count for the compatible/incompatible contrast from 6 to 4 (VIM-2, calmodulin, PPM1D,
   NUDT15).

**Caveat 2 is the coverage-collapse lesson that shaped the PTM study's design.** A
one-member chemical family cannot produce a family-preserving non-WT substitution, so the
contrast that the whole test rests on is empty before a single score is computed — and
that emptiness is invisible until the positions are enumerated. The PTM project therefore
sizes every family *before* committing to a locus, and the reconnaissance
(`docs/recon/recon_ptm_disulfide_results.md`, §3.1) found the same collapse waiting in two
of the loci the brief had assumed were safe: the phospho-**Tyr** site ({Y}, 1 member) and
the glycosylation sequon **Asn** acceptor ({N}, 1 member — measured: 2,145 available
substitutions across 117 covered Asn positions, **0** of them modification-preserving).
The surviving loci are exactly those with a family of size ≥ 2: Ser/Thr phosphosites
({S,T}) and the sequon **+2** position ({S,T,C}).
